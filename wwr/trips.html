Our Rafting Trips

Every trip is led by experienced guides and includes all the safety gear you need. Pick the adventure that matches your group and your appetite for adrenaline.

Trip Details

Family Float

A relaxed half-day trip on gentle water with small rapids, perfect for first-time rafters and children. Your guide shares local wildlife and river history along the way, and there is time for a swim stop.

Canyon Classic

Our most popular full-day adventure. Paddle through exciting Class III and IV rapids, enjoy a riverside lunch, and learn paddle commands that keep your crew working together as a team.

Three-Day Expedition

Spend three days and two nights on the river, running Class III to V rapids by day and camping on quiet riverbanks at night. Meals, camp gear, and guides are all included.
